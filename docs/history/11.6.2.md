# STEP11.6.2 — Noble focus, aligned PFT text and correct stereo PDA

## Before

STEP11.6.1 added Noble system-font APIs, but Maze still stopped on missing focus rendering while Duel Of Shadows exposed PFT alignment and stereo PDA decoding defects.

## Changes

`lockFocus()` and `unlockFocus()` were added, PFT glyph offsets were aligned against the complete font stream, stereo IMA ADPCM nibble decoding was corrected, and slow Lua frames now yield safely to avoid starving the watchdog. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## Result

Maze can progress past the Noble focus API, Duel Of Shadows regains its missing text and clean audio, and over-budget Lua frames no longer trigger IDLE0 watchdog starvation.
