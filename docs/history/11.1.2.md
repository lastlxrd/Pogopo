# STEP11.1.2 — Celeste performance fix

## Before

Celeste worked, but each frame took about 190 ms and the screen appeared to update at only 1–2 FPS.

## Changes

The catch-up scheduler was removed, framebuffer scaling was simplified and sprite, image and final blit paths were optimized.

## Result

After building and flashing, Celeste still ran at roughly 5 FPS and remained effectively unplayable.
