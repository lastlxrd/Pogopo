# STEP13.2 — Interactive Power Outro

## Before

STEP13.1 polished the launcher and quick panel but still used the previous power-animation behavior.

## Changes

The final 25-frame Intro and Outro artwork was integrated, the quick panel was cleaned up, and Power hold detection now plays the Outro forward before shutdown or reverses it cleanly when the hold is released early. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## Result

Power input now cleanly distinguishes taps from holds, shutdown completes only after the full Outro, and cancelled shutdowns restore the interrupted screen without breaking Game Boy resume behavior.
