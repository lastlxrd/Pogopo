# STEP11.6.32 — polyphonic samples, scene backgrounds and transition safety

This step follows hardware testing of Loopsy and XTRIS on STEP11.6.31. The
changes are runtime-wide compatibility fixes rather than title-specific asset
substitutions.

## Audio

- Four short PCM sample voices can now overlap. Starting a navigation or game
  sound no longer truncates the preceding waveform at an arbitrary sample,
  which previously produced clicks that could resemble the fallback/error
  sound.
- Sample and music playback now use linear interpolation when converting a
  package's source rate to the hardware mixer rate. This removes much of the
  aliasing heard in Loopsy's music and sound effects.
- `playdate.sound.channel:setVolume()` now propagates channel gain to sources
  already in the channel and to sources added later. XTRIS's intended SFX and
  music levels are therefore respected.

## Graphics and scene lifecycle

- Circled A/B fallback glyphs scale with the active compiled font instead of
  remaining fixed at 7×7 pixels. Loopsy's menu prompts now match the nearby
  text size.
- An obsolete sprite background callback is cleared when a scene removes its
  entire display list. PogoDate redraws a full immediate-mode frame, unlike
  the SDK's retained dirty-region renderer, so preserving the callback painted
  XTRIS's menu dither over every subsequent gameplay frame.
- New scenes remain free to install their own background callback. The XTRIS
  title, level selection and result screens retain their intended dither while
  gameplay returns to a white background.

## Optional achievements and coroutines

- Calls to the common `achievements` helper are protected at the service
  boundary when a public/non-Catalog PDX omits an achievement definition.
  Configured achievements still use the game's original implementation and
  unrelated errors are rethrown.
- XTRIS 1.1.2 contains a call to the absent optional `inner-peace`
  achievement. The helper raised inside its result-transition coroutine; the
  game ignored the failed `coroutine.resume()` and left the `TIME` animation
  permanently active. The optional call now reports a warning and the result
  screen opens normally.

## Validation

- XTRIS: 3000 frames through a complete timed round and result screen, zero
  runtime errors, zero fallback sounds; packaged SFX and music were found.
- XTRIS gameplay capture has a white background while menu/result captures
  retain their local dither patterns.
- Existing STEP11.6.31 argument, resource-path, coroutine and fatal-audio
  compatibility remains enabled.
