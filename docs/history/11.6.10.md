# STEP11.6.10 — Maze completion and Duel boss-fight polish

## Before

STEP11.6.9 corrected Maze-specific controls and moved its Level Complete frames into the PSRAM pool, but the completion flow still had an accidental START-to-A path and both Maze transitions and Duel boss combat retained visual and collision issues.

## Changes

Maze now disables the START-to-A alias only while it owns input, sparse LevelComplete images render only their real content bounds with a reused composition target, and Duel gains flip-correct scaled sprites, frame-size tracking and swept collision handling. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## Result

Maze keeps pixel-identical Level Complete output while its host regression drops from roughly 2.53 s to 0.31 s, and Duel Of Shadows now renders left-facing movement correctly and avoids the previous boss-fight position jumps and collision ejections.
